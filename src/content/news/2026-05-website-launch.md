After plenty of "we should probably have a website" — we now do. You're looking at it.

The plan is to keep this simple: somewhere new players can find out **when we play, where, and how to join**, and somewhere we can drop the occasional update from the courts.

## What's here

- **Sessions** — Sunday and Tuesday times, who they're for, and where to find us.
- **Join us** — three steps to getting onto our Spond group, where all sessions are booked.
- **News** — what you're reading. Match reports, socials, club gossip will land here.

## What to expect next

We'll add proper photos from sessions soon, and the committee page once we've taken some passable headshots.

If you've got something to share — a match report, a great rally, a photo from a session — send it to
[hello@firstandlastpickleball.co.uk](mailto:hello@firstandlastpickleball.co.uk).

See you on court.
