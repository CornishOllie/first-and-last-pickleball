There's a new class at GO! St Just. **PickleFit** is hosted by Alex Rowe, our Level 1 coach, and it runs **every Monday, 6 to 7pm**.

It combines the fun of pickleball with a fast-paced workout, designed to improve your fitness, footwork, reactions, coordination and accuracy. It's for anyone, from complete beginners to pros.

Each session starts with a dynamic warm-up, then a mixture of:

- Target-hitting and accuracy challenges
- Fast-paced rallies and reaction drills
- Footwork and court coverage skills
- Individual, partner and team activities

## The details

- **Where:** GO! St Just Sports & Fitness Centre, Cape Cornwall Road, St Just, TR19 7JX
- **When:** every Monday, 6 to 7pm
- **Cost:** £7 a class, or save with sessions in bulk. Included with GO! memberships.
- **Booking:** on the Gymflow app or at [go-stjust.org](https://www.go-stjust.org/). Walk-ins are welcome, but booking a place is recommended. You can also call 01736 787856.

PickleFit is run by GO! St Just rather than through our Spond group, so book through them.
