Following the buzz from Drill & Thrill, there's another chance for competition across the club on **Saturday 21 November**.

It's for **all abilities**, and it's limited to **32 entries**. The day is a random doubles round-robin with medals, followed by a fixed doubles knockout with more medals on offer. It's designed for maximum enjoyment and fun.

You enter on your own. Partners are allocated after the round-robin, so there's nothing to arrange and no need to find a partner.

Neil will set it up on Spond in the coming days, so everyone will get a notification. Keep an eye out for it.

Full details are on the [competitions page](/competitions/2026-11-club-doubles).
