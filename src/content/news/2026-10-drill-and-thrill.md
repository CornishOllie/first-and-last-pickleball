On Saturday 10 October, World Pickleball Day, 16 of us filled the hall at GO! St Just for **Drill & Thrill**: two hours of coaching followed by a doubles tournament. It was run by [Land's End Pickleball](https://www.landsendpickleball.co.uk) and sponsored by [UK Pickleball Shop](https://pickleball.co.uk), and it was a cracking day.

![Everyone with their racquets at the net, between the UK Pickleball Shop and Land's End Pickleball banners](/img/dt-group.jpg)

## The coaching

Alex Rowe, our Level 1 coach, had put together a set of drills where each one led into the next. We worked through:

1. **The serve**
2. **The return**
3. **The third shot drop**
4. **Dinking**

Every drill had a practice part first, then a competitive part to put it under a bit of pressure. By the time we got to dinking, the serve, return and drop we had just practised were already feeding straight into it, which is how a point actually goes. It made sense of the whole sequence.

![Alex Rowe, our coach for the day, between the two sponsor banners](/img/dt-alex.jpg)

Alex also let us shape the morning with a pre-event drill poll on Spond, so the session covered what people wanted to work on.

## The tournament

After two hours of drills and a short break, it was straight into a round-robin of doubles. The top four pairs went through to the semi-finals:

- Vicky and Alice v Ollie and Megan
- Paul and Luke v Helen and Jacquie

Ollie and Megan and Paul and Luke won through to the final, with Helen and Jacquie playing Vicky and Alice for third.

![Doubles in full flow on the St Just courts](/img/dt-rally.jpg)

## The final

It was a belter. Luke and Paul beat Ollie and Megan **11-10** in a very competitive match, with one point between the pairs at the end. An all-time classic.

![Luke and Paul with their gold medals](/img/dt-gold.jpg)

![Ollie and Megan with their silver medals](/img/dt-silver.jpg)

Helen and Jacquie took bronze, beating Vicky and Alice in the third place play-off.

![Helen and Jacquie with their bronze medals](/img/dt-bronze.jpg)

![The medallists with Alex in front of the UK Pickleball Shop banner](/img/dt-podium.jpg)
