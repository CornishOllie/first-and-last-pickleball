Following the AGM, and some research and discussion between Neil and Alex, we've agreed to go ahead with a **self-evaluation guide**. Anyone at the club can use it to work out their playing level.

You'll find it on our new [Find your level](/find-your-level) page, with a printable PDF too. It covers five levels, from Beginner to Advanced, with measurable criteria for each and a quick self-test you can try in practice.

## Some things to know

- **It is not a mandatory test.** It's there to help you assess your own level.
- **Consistency counts.** When you work through the checklist, think about what you do reliably, rather than what you might achieve occasionally.
- **It forms part of the Wednesday assessment.** The guide will be part of the Intermediate Plus assessment, alongside things like following the code of conduct and sportsmanship.
- **Coaching.** In addition to his existing programme, Alex is considering coaching sessions for people who don't yet meet enough of the criteria but want to improve and move up a level.

We expect it will start a few conversations, and Neil and Alex are both happy to answer questions.
