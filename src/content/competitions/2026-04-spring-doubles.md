A cracking turnout for the first championship of the year. Eight pairs played a full round-robin
across the morning, with every pair guaranteed seven games.

Dave T and Sarah M were imperious — seven wins from seven, dropping only a handful of games all
day. The real drama was the scrap for second: John B & Pete R and Meg & Ollie finished level on
points, with second place decided on head-to-head.

Thanks to everyone who played, to the volunteers who kept the score sheets straight, and to whoever
brought the lemon drizzle.
